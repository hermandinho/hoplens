example-express-app

An Express 5 app with seeded problems for Hoplens. Open /__hoplens (dev script only).

  • /api/health (baseline)
  • /api/users/2/orders/3 (N+1)
  • /api/dashboard (sequential queries)
  • /api/products/7 (duplicate query)
  • /api/report (~2 MB JSON)
  • /api/flaky (fails ~1 in 3)
  • /api/boom (sync throw)
  • /files/docs/guide.md (wildcard)
